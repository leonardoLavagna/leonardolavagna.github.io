I'm a [mathematician](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SeZ_gbBPUw0) specialized in [data science](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tN5KIUNfYGA&t=1s) and [quantum computing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RQWpF2Gb-gU). I work at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/pagina-strutturale/home) in the [Department of Information Engineering, Electronics and Telecommunications (DIET)](https://web.uniroma1.it/dip_diet/en) in the [Neural Network Systems & Applications Laboratory (NESYA)](https://sites.google.com/view/nesya) multidisciplinary lab. Before starting my academic journey I've worked as an IT specialist and as a data scientist for a consulting firm in Rome. Recently I've started my own company [LittleBulb](https://www.littlebulb.it/en/home-eng/) to encourage, within the framework of a commercially sustainable "[web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deschooling_Society)", the [knowledge transfer between academia, industry and society at large](https://www.uniroma1.it/en/pagina/sapienzas-commitment-outreach-activities-and-fourth-mission), but also to bring industrial standards, best practices and projects into the [ivory tower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivory_tower). Here I share some parts of my work as well as those projects I carry out in my free time and which are helping me grow, both personally and professionally.
