On this page, you can find materials related to the recitation sessions and course projects for [Quantum Computing and Neural Networks](https://sites.google.com/uniroma1.it/massimopanella-eng/teaching/qcnn?authuser=0), a course offered within the Master's Degree programmes in Electronics Engineering and Telecommunications Engineering, taught by Professor Massimo Panella, PhD, with the support of the [NESYA Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/nesya-it).

In particular, this page provides supplementary materials covering selected topics in quantum computing, including complementary theoretical concepts, laboratory sessions, project ideas, and related resources.
