Our contribution on the relationship between [QAOA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_optimization_algorithms#Quantum_approximate_optimization_algorithm) layers
and [MaxCut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_cut) structure via "agnostic" variational solvers where the problem structure is controllably removed
and the corresponding solutions analyzed (in terms of cost landscapes and quality) has been published in 
Applied Mathematics and Statistics. The article is available [here](https://www.sciltp.com/journals/ams/articles/2610005354). Code available 
[here](https://github.com/leonardoLavagna/maxcut-vqa-architectures) and related blog post [here](https://lavagnaleo.wordpress.com/2026/04/11/some-considerations-on-possible-enhancements-of-the-qaoa-ansatz-via-agnostic-multi-parameterized-layers-and-advanced-parameters-update/).
