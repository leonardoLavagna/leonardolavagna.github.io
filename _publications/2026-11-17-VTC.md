Within the framework of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems, this paper addresses the problem of optimizing velocity profiles matching green waves for connected vehicles travelling along fixed trajectories. We propose a mathematical model and a quantum algorithm, that incorporate vehicle kinematics, physical constraints, and traffic light states, comparing theoretical benchmarks against real-world data from an instrumented trolleybus running in Milan (Italy).
